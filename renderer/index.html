90s Craig Edit Booth — Guide

VHS Editor finds the commercials in a digitized tape, lets you fix up the picture and sound, and exports either a clean commercial-free copy or the commercials themselves as vertical clips for TikTok / Reels.

Quick start

  1. Open a capture — click Open capture… (top-right) or drag a video onto the player. A small preview builds in the background so scrubbing is fast — watch the badge at the top-left of the video. Detection and export always use your full-quality original, never the preview.
  2. Detect commercials — click Detect commercials. It scans for fade-to-black + audio silence (the marks between show and ads) and splits the tape into clips.
  3. Review — clips appear on the timeline and in the list, colored green = keep / red = cut. Click one to play it, and fix any mistakes (see Editing).
  4. Pick a preset — in Export, click 📺 Clean tape or 🎬 Social clips.
  5. Export — choose an output folder if you like, then Export. Files save next to your source by default.

The two jobs

  • 📺 Clean tape — exports the kept program as one merged file, commercials removed.
  • 🎬 Social clips — exports the cut commercials, reframed to vertical 9:16 with a blurred background, as separate ready-to-post files.

Editing clips

Detection gets you most of the way; fine-tune the rest:

  • Click a clip to select and preview it. Click its keep/cut tag (or press K) to flip it.
  • Drag the yellow boundary handles on the timeline to move where a clip starts/ends.
  • S split the selected clip at the playhead · M / Shift+M merge with the previous/next clip.
  • I / O snap the selected clip's in / out point to the playhead.

Detect settings

  • Black sensitivity — how dark a frame must be to count as a break. Raise it if breaks are missed.
  • Silence threshold — how quiet the audio must drop at a break.
  • Min commercial gap — ignores tiny gaps (noise).
  • Max commercial length — clips shorter than this are guessed to be commercials.

Restore (optional)

  • Enhance — denoise + re-sharpen by tape speed (SP / LP / EP). Use heavier presets for older, noisier tapes.
  • Correct color — brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma, plus Red/Green/Blue balance to remove color casts.
  • Audio drift — shift the audio to fix lip-sync (positive = audio later).

Export settings

  • What to export — kept program, or the cut commercials.
  • Frame / Fill — keep source shape or reframe for social; Fill handles the leftover space (blurred background looks best).
  • Resolution · Frame rate · Quality — output size and encoding (Archive → Low).
  • Export as — a custom filename. One merged file vs Separate clips.

Keyboard

Space play/pause · ←→ step frame (Shift = 1 sec) · Home/End · S split · M merge · I/O set in-out · K keep/cut

Tip: hover the i icons around the app for quick explanations of each control.
